# Writing the hook text (Glorious Goddess / MariaTalksTech)

The hook text is the sentence in the dark box for the first seconds of the video. Its one job: make a viewer scrolling
with the sound off stay for the next line. Write 3 options per video, each with a one-line `why`, and render with the
best one. Maria picks or edits in the Video Editor's Hook tab.

## What holds up (research, 2026-11-10)
Short-form guides broadly agree on the points below. Most of the numbers floating around them (for example "85% watch
muted" or "+340% reach") can't be traced to a primary source, so don't quote them to her. The patterns:
- **Text on screen from the first frame.** Many viewers scroll muted, and Instagram reports "skip rate", the share who
  leave in the first 3 s. A spoken-only hook misses the muted ones.
- **Short.** About 12 words max, 2-3 lines in the box. A hook that's hard to read in 2 seconds loses.
- **Add to the first spoken line, don't read it back.** The text sets up the stakes or the question; her voice starts the
  story. When the text repeats her first sentence word for word, nothing new pulls the viewer in.
- **Curiosity gap with a fair payoff.** Show the outcome or the question and withhold the answer. The answer must come
  soon: a payoff 20 s into a 35 s video feels like a trick. Note in the `why` when the payoff arrives (e.g. "pays off at 0:12").
- **Call out the exact viewer and situation.** Specific beats general ("Planning date #3 yourself?" beats "Dating tips").
- **Her face in the first frame, talking right away.** Already true for her videos; the box sits over her chest so her
  face stays visible.
- **Test.** Hook styles can be compared by posting otherwise identical content and watching 3-second hold, average
  watch time and saves. If she ever shares her Instagram insights, use them to learn which formula works for HER
  audience, and record what you learn below.

## Formulas that fit her voice (direct, first-person, story-first, women 40+)
1. **Situation + promise:** name the exact moment she's describing, promise the meaning. "Two dates, then silence.
   *Here's what it means.*"
2. **Call-out + warning:** the viewer about to make a move. "Planning date #3 yourself? *Watch this first*"
3. **Either/or curiosity:** a question with two answers where she reveals which. "Girlfriend bucket or *options bucket?*"
4. **Mid-story drop-in (storytime):** start inside the story, with a detail that raises a question. "A man asked me to
   drive 30 mins to meet him for coffee 🤨" (her own, from before 2026-11). Strong for her car stories.
5. **Bold reframe / contrarian:** flip what the viewer believes. "He's not confused. *He just didn't choose you.*"
   (Use when the reframe IS the video; it gives the answer away, so the rest must add the "why" or the "what now".)
6. **Mistake / red flag:** "The red flag most women miss *on the first date*". Specific behaviour beats vague "red flags".
7. **Number + payoff** (for list videos with cards): "3 texts that *kill attraction*".
For MTT (AI for non-technical women): same formulas, outcome-first ("I let AI plan my week. *Here's what happened*").

## Rules for her hooks
- Use her words and her world: dating after 40, leaving, starting over, single and happy. No generic "coach" lines
  ("Ladies, listen up!", "You need to hear this", "Watch till the end").
- Stay true to the video: the hook has to pay off. Never promise something she doesn't say.
- No clickbait words (SHOCKING, insane), no ALL CAPS, at most one emoji (at the end, matching her tone: 🙄 😑 🤨 😅 🐆).
- `*accent*` on the 1-3 words that carry the emotion or the promise (blush italic). Use `|` so an accent phrase
  isn't split across lines.
- Sentence case. Punctuation where it helps reading ("Two dates, then silence.").
- Timing: from 0 until the end of the sentence where the setup lands (often the 2nd-3rd sentence, 4-10 s).
- If she gives a hook or title when dropping the video, that's the pick; still offer 2 alternatives.
- Thumbnail headline (step 3) and the hook can share the idea but shouldn't be word-for-word identical: the cover is
  the title on her grid, the hook is the first line of the watch.
