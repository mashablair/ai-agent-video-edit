# Glorious visuals (step 2 of 2)

## Steps
1. **Words.** `$PY _agent/polish.py words Videos/<name>/<clean file> ~/vp/<name>_g/words.json` writes the clean video's word timings `[{w,s,e}]` (the project's `words`) and prints them indexed. Merge ASR splits like "Bread"+"crumbing"; fix words the ASR misheard (compare with `<name>_transcript.txt`); hide leftover fillers from captions with `"hide":true` (audio stays). A comment keyword in quotes keeps its punctuation outside the quotes: `comment "choose",` never `"choose,"` (the quoted word triggers her ManyChat automation, so it must read exactly as typed; glorious.py also fixes this on load). Write the project (step 7) far enough to hold the words, then run `$PY $G numbers <project>` (`--dry` previews). Always do this; she approved the result on 1030 (2026-10-31), so don't re-ask: prices, years and percents she says become digits on one caption in the `num` style ("nine and a half million." -> "$9.5 million.", "twenty twenty five" -> "2025", "ten percent" -> "10%", "20 and 40 million dollars" -> "$20 and $40 million."). Amounts already in digits get the same style if no keyword style is set yet. Read what it prints: "left as spoken" lists amounts with no clear unit, so fix those by hand if they're prices ("two million" a viewer would read as dollars). Counts stay words ("one of the boats", "two kids", "three-story"); ages stay as she said them. Run it before picking keywords.
2. **Look at it.** Contact sheet every 2-3s; note where her face is and any on-screen text. If her video already has captions burned in, set `captions.enabled:false` and keep the hook text, accents, zooms and B-roll (1108-8).
3. **Pick keywords** (her taste: hand-picked, ~1 per 3-4s, never automatic). Set `word.emph` to one of: `neg` (Inter Tight Bold Italic in NEGATIVE mode — letters show the inverted video; use for the topic word and strong nouns: breadcrumbing, intermittent, committing, dependent), `serif` (Instrument Serif Italic, elegant: relationship, illusion, treatment, woman), `script` (yellow brush script, warm/value words: choice, standard, dating, God forbid), `yellow` (mustard bold, punchy: leaving?, out, disappearing, walk away), `num` (amber bold, prices/years/percents only; set by `numbers`, colour `captions.gNumColor`). Prefer the last word of a sentence; vary styles; don't repeat the same keyword word with the same style back-to-back.
4. **B-roll.** Read CATALOG.md incl. the usage log. Glorious B-roll comes in bursts on lines that are visual (e.g. "one day he's there / one day he's not / hot and cold"), 1.2-2s per clip in a burst, single slots 2-3s elsewhere, ~20-30% of runtime. Literal matches; her own clips first, stock/AI only when nothing fits (max 1-2 stock). Never a clip from the most recent usage-log entry (CHECK this before rendering), never one twice, never photo-me-and-bond unless asked. Keep her face for the opening question, thesis lines, punchlines and the last line. Slot options: `"headline":"there"` or `"hot|COLD"` (big word over the B-roll; 2-part = serif line over big sans), `"reveal":true` (framed photo on a blurred copy that grows to full screen; good for a scene-setting line).
5. **Cards** (list items she counts out): `project.tiles=[{a,b,num,text}]` — the Captions card: wide light panel low in the frame, translucent strip on top, bold gray "0 1", label in gray. Follow her numbering in the video; if Captions-style sub-steps are used, say so in the reply.
6. **Hook text and accent words** (changed 2026-11-10 at her request; before that the hook was one big word behind her).
   - **Hook text** (`project.hookText`): her hook sentence in a dark box in front of her from the first frame until the setup has landed (usually 4-10 s; set `b` to the end of a sentence + 0.25 s). This is the real hook: it has to stop a muted scroller. **Write 3 options** from the transcript, following `hooks.md` in this skill's folder (read it every time), each with a one-line `why`, and render with your top pick. If she gave you a hook or title when she dropped the video, that one is the pick (still list 2 alternatives). Format: `{"enabled":true, "options":[{"text","why"}x3], "text":<the pick>, "a":0, "b":<s>, "style":"serif", "size":0.07, "y":0.56, "box":0.74}`. `*stars*` = blush italic accent words, `|` = forced line break (use it so an accent phrase isn't split across lines), emoji work. `style`: `serif` (default: the thumbnail's look, DM Serif Display cream in a stepped dark box), `sans` (her older look: Poppins in one rounded box), `card` (old cream card). **Always place it with `$PY $G placehook <project>` once the text and timing are set** (her rule, 2026-11-10: the box must never cover her face; at y 0.56 it covered her chin). It tracks her face over the whole hook window on the final framing (zooms included) and puts the box just below her chin, or above her head when there's plenty of room under Instagram's top bar; if the captions would touch the box it sets `capY`, the caption height while the hook shows (they go back to normal after). It stores the measurements in `hookText.place` (+ `auto:true`) so the editor re-places the box when she switches option or size. Check by looking at frames across the whole hook (the opening pull-back and any tight framing included), with `$PY $G hookbox <project> /tmp/x.png` for the box alone. She switches options, edits the text, look, size, box opacity, height and timing in the Video Editor's **Hook** tab.
   - **Accent words** (`project.accents=[{text,a,b}]`, shared look in `project.accentStyle={y,size,style,color,outlineOn,outline,outlineColor,shadowOn,shadow,shadowBlur,shadowOpacity}`): the huge Inter Tight Black words that sit BEHIND her (Robust Video Matting cut-out composited on top, through the same zooms), rise in ("sunrise", 0.65 s) and fade out after `b`. Put them on the big moments through the video (punchline, core reframe, closing line), 1.5-2.5 s each, as many as the video earns (no fixed count; roughly one per 15-25 s), never under B-roll (it needs her face), not on the first seconds (the hook box is there). One or two words from what she says at that moment, one line ("Not confused", "Options?", "Choose"); a two-line accent pushes its second line into her head. While an accent shows, the framing eases back to wide (renderer and editor), so her head sits low enough. `y` and `size` come from `$PY $G placehook <project>` (or `placeaccents`): it finds the top of her hair with the person cut-out while each accent shows and puts the word's baseline a quarter of its letter height below it, so her head covers the bottom slightly; it shrinks the size if there's no room above (her rule, 2026-11-11: in 1108-9 a fixed y 0.085, set by eye for 1108-4, left the words floating far above her head). Never set y by hand from another video; `size` 0.24 by default (auto-shrinks to 94% of the width); `style` `neg` (inverted picture), `white` or `color`. Check every accent on a frame: the word must read. She adds, removes and retimes them (and changes their look) in the Hook tab; the timeline shows them as purple marks, the hook as a burgundy one.
   - Old projects with a single `project.hook` word still render: it's read as one accent (a `card` hook becomes the hook text). The editor converts it the same way on load.
7. **Write the project** `Videos/<name>/editor files/<name>_broll_project.json` (the editor's format): `{version:1, video:"Videos/<name>/<clean file>", duration, fps, rendered, status, savedAt, words, slots:[{a,b,clip,from,line,headline?,reveal?}], tiles, zooms, zoomAnchor:[0.515,0.5], whipAt:[...], hookText:{...}, accents:[...], accentStyle:{...}, captions:{enabled:true, engine:"glorious", preset:"glorious", color:"#FCFCFC", y:0.733, yB:0.70, gLine:0.72, gPad:0.06, gScriptColor:"#F2EC2E", gYellowColor:"#D9C933", gSizes:{small:.052,big:.084,neg:.102,serif:.14,script:.15,yellow:.12}, gOutlineOn:true, gOutline:0.0065, gShadowOn:true, gShadow:0.009, start:0}}` (if `_agent/caption-presets.json` has a "glorious" entry, use those caption values instead; it is the source of truth). Her default since 2026-11-04 (1103-1): a bit smaller, lower on the frame, more shadow and a thin border so captions are easier to read. Leave `speed` out (1x) unless she asks.
8. **Sound effects** (added 2026-11-11; she approved the Mixkit set's volume on the 1108-4 demo): after the accents, B-roll, cards and zooms are final, run `$PY $G sfx <project>` (`--dry` previews). It writes `project.sfx={enabled,volume,cues:[{t,sound,vol,why}]}`: a whoosh as each accent rises in, a swish on whip zooms, a soft whoosh into each B-roll burst, a pop on cards, a ka-ching on money amounts, a ding on the comment keyword; at most one per second. `assemble` mixes them under her voice (sounds and level matches in `_agent/sfx/sfx.json`; the Mixkit files are not in the public git repo, `_agent/sfx/fetch_mixkit.py` re-downloads them). Re-run it after changing accents/B-roll/zooms. The Video Editor keeps the cues and moves them with re-cuts, but has no Sounds controls yet.
8. **Zooms:** set `whipAt` to 3-5 big lines (~every 20-25s: "And here's the thing", the core reframe, the punchline), then run `$PY $G autozoom <project>`. It plans the approved Captions-style zooms (see below).
9. **Render:** `$PY $G plan <project>` prints the caption pages and the chunk count; read the pages like a viewer and fix keywords/words if a page reads badly. Then, with `WD=~/vp/<name>_g`, `for k in $(seq 0 $((N-1))); do $PY $G chunk <project> $WD $k; done && $PY $G assemble <project> $WD $WD/final.mp4` (each chunk makes the cut-outs its accent words need; the frame count must match the "expected" it prints).
10. **Check:** `$PY $G sheet <project> $WD/final.mp4 $WD/sheet.jpg` makes a contact sheet of the hook text, every accent word, the middle of every B-roll slot, every card and her face across the video; view it. Look for: the hook box readable and clear of her face, every accent word readable above/behind her head, B-roll clips that match their lines, no caption line wider than the frame, keyword lines that look right, nothing over her face, cards readable. Optionally measure zooms by ORB-matching final vs clean frames (scale per frame) and make sure there are no single-frame jumps.
11. **Deliver** `Videos/<name>/<name>_glorious.mp4` (next _v2, _v3 if it exists), `<name>_glorious_list.txt` (hook text with the 3 options and their whys, accent words with their lines, slots, keywords, zooms, cards, B-roll %, speed), update the project file (`status:"rendered"`, `rendered`), and add/replace the usage-log line in CATALOG.md (`- <date>, <video file stem> (<topic>, Glorious): clip, clip, ...`). Move superseded versions into `_agent/tmp/superseded/`. Reply briefly with a what-she-says/what's-on-screen table and that she can tweak everything in `Editors/Video Editor.html` (reload it first), then say "render my edits for <name>". When done, clear `~/vp` work dirs of finished videos.

## Speed (added 2026-10-21)
`project.speed` (1 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3; missing = 1x) is set by her with the Speed menu next to the play button in the editor; the preview plays at that speed and shows the final length. The renderer keeps every timing (captions, zooms, B-roll, hook) in the clean video's timeline, speeds up each chunk at the very end (cumulative frame rounding keeps chunks in sync) and the audio with atempo (pitch stays natural). Default is 1x: never speed a video up on your own.

## The Glorious captions (renderer + editor preview use identical rules)
- Inter Tight Bold #FCFCFC, no outline/shadow/box by default, centered, tight tracking (-1.7%) and leading; each word slides in from the left and fades up as she says it; first word of each caption supersized.
- Pages: split at punctuation and pauses >0.5s; balanced chunks of at most 5 words; a keyword ends its page and sits alone on line 2; a keyword that opens a phrase stands alone; never a lone word left after a keyword; lines wrap at `gLine` (72% of width) and a page never exceeds 2 lines (it splits). Single long words shrink to fit inside `gPad`. When line 2 is small text the line gap is wider so it never touches the big first word.
- Editor options she uses: sizes per style, side padding, line width, colors (main, script, mustard), outline and shadow (not on negative words), height on face / on B-roll; presets (Bold Pop, Clean, Elegant, Boxed, Glorious) show their names and can be saved back with "Save changes to …" (stored in `_agent/caption-presets.json`).

## The zooms (approved 2026-10-21; modeled on the Captions video at 0:21-0:25)
- A new framing on almost every caption page while her face is on screen (~every 1.5s): wide 1.0 / medium 1.12 / tight 1.24, keyword pages tight, never the same twice in a row, at least 1.1s apart.
- Each change snaps in 0.22s, or instantly on a jump cut within 0.35s (hides the cut); then the framing creeps in slowly (+1.5%/s).
- Whips on big lines: one-frame-ish white flash (~55%, 2 frames) + 1.55x snapping back to medium over 0.8s with the measured curve [1, .951, .805, .369, .147, .065, .027, .005, 0]. Opening: soft 1.35x pull-back, no flash.
- No zoom changes under B-roll; after B-roll she comes back on a fresh wide/medium framing. Zoom is centered at (0.515, 0.5).
- Lessons: rare/slow zooms (every 8-10s) and subtle drifts were rejected — the fast per-phrase rhythm is the point. When studying a reference video built on an earlier edit of ours, compare against OUR clean video, not the intermediate (the intermediate already contained zooms and cancels them out).
