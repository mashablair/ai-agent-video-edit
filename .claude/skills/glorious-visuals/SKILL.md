# Glorious visuals (step 2 of 2)

Input: a video already cleaned by step 1 (polish-talking-video), e.g. `Videos/<name>/<name>_clean.mp4`. Output: the finished video with the "Glorious" look, plus a project file she can tweak in `Editors/B-roll Editor.html`. Use this instead of add-broll-to-video when she wants the Glorious style (or "all the visuals"). Don't ask her to approve a plan first; deliver, then she edits.

The look was copied from a Captions-app video she loves and refined with her on 2026-10-20/21. Everything below is approved; don't re-ask about style.

## Where things are (her Mac, via device_bash)
- Videos: `$HOME/mnt/AI agent video edit/` (`Drop raw videos here/` for her raw videos, `Videos/<name>/` for everything generated for that video). B-roll library: `$HOME/mnt/b-roll collection/` (clips/, _catalog/CATALOG.md, clips.json). If a folder isn't connected, request it (device_request_folder_access) or ask her to add it.
- `_agent/glorious.py` = the renderer (captions, B-roll, zooms, cards, headlines, hook word, speed). `_agent/fonts/` holds the Glorious fonts (GLSans, GLSansItalic, GLSansMedium, GLSansBlack, GLSerifItalic, GLScript + cal.json) and fonts.json for the editor. `_agent/rvm_mask.py` + `_agent/rvm_mobilenetv3_fp32.onnx` = person cut-out for the hook (needs `pip3 install --user onnxruntime` on a fresh machine). `_agent/caption-presets.json` = her saved preset tweaks (the editor writes it; apply her saved "glorious" version if present). Backups of earlier versions are in `_agent/tmp/`.
- Each device_bash call is capped at 180s; renders go in ~20s chunks. Work dirs in `~/vp/<name>`, never in her folders.

## Steps
1. **Setup + words.** `mkdir -p ~/vidpolish && cp "$HOME/mnt/AI agent video edit/_agent/polish.py" ~/vidpolish/ && python3 ~/vidpolish/polish.py setup`. Get word timings of the CLEAN video with polish's `verbatim_words` (import polish.py and call `verbatim_words(<16k mono wav of the clean video>)`), save as the project words `[{w,s,e}]`. Merge ASR splits like "Bread"+"crumbing"; hide leftover fillers from captions with `"hide":true` (audio stays).
2. **Look at it.** Contact sheet every 2-3s; note where her face is and any on-screen text.
3. **Pick keywords** (her taste: hand-picked, ~1 per 3-4s, never automatic). Set `word.emph` to one of: `neg` (Inter Tight Bold Italic in NEGATIVE mode — letters show the inverted video; use for the topic word and strong nouns: breadcrumbing, intermittent, committing, dependent), `serif` (Instrument Serif Italic, elegant: relationship, illusion, treatment, woman), `script` (yellow brush script, warm/value words: choice, standard, dating, God forbid), `yellow` (mustard bold, punchy: leaving?, out, disappearing, walk away). Prefer the last word of a sentence; vary styles; don't repeat the same keyword word with the same style back-to-back.
4. **B-roll.** Read CATALOG.md incl. the usage log. Glorious B-roll comes in bursts on lines that are visual (e.g. "one day he's there / one day he's not / hot and cold"), 1.2-2s per clip in a burst, single slots 2-3s elsewhere, ~20-30% of runtime. Literal matches; her own clips first, stock/AI only when nothing fits (max 1-2 stock). Never a clip from the most recent usage-log entry (CHECK this before rendering), never one twice, never photo-me-and-bond unless asked. Keep her face for the opening question, thesis lines, punchlines and the last line. Slot options: `"headline":"there"` or `"hot|COLD"` (big word over the B-roll; 2-part = serif line over big sans), `"reveal":true` (framed photo on a blurred copy that grows to full screen; good for a scene-setting line).
5. **Cards** (list items she counts out): `project.tiles=[{a,b,num,text}]` — the Captions card: wide light panel low in the frame, translucent strip on top, bold gray "0 1", label in gray. Follow her numbering in the video; if Captions-style sub-steps are used, say so in the reply.
6. **Hook word** (added 2026-10-21, modeled on a Captions "$8M" example she liked): `project.hook={enabled, text, a, b, y, size, style, color}` — a huge Inter Tight Black word that sits BEHIND her (a Robust Video Matting cut-out of her is composited back on top, and it goes through the same zooms), rises slightly from below while fading in ("sunrise", 0.65s) and fades out after `b`. `style`: `neg` (default: letters show the inverted picture), `white`, or `color` (+`color`). Defaults: `a:0`, `b` = end of the first sentence + 0.2s, `y:0.22`, `size:0.34` (auto-shrinks to fit the width); `|` makes a line break (e.g. "Bread|crumbing" stacks behind her head). Pick 1-2 punchy words for the topic/promise of the video. She's still settling on a hook style: set it up, and tell her she can change the word or switch it off with "Show a big hook word" in the editor's Captions tab. The renderer makes/refreshes the cut-out automatically (hookmask.mp4 in the work dir; ~6s for 3s of video).
7. **Write the project** `Videos/<name>/editor files/<name>_broll_project.json` (the editor's format): `{version:1, video:"Videos/<name>/<clean file>", duration, fps, rendered, status, savedAt, words, slots:[{a,b,clip,from,line,headline?,reveal?}], tiles, zooms, zoomAnchor:[0.515,0.5], whipAt:[...], hook:{...}, captions:{enabled:true, engine:"glorious", preset:"glorious", color:"#FCFCFC", y:0.635, yB:0.70, gLine:0.72, gPad:0.06, gScriptColor:"#F2EC2E", gYellowColor:"#D9C933", gSizes:{small:.068,big:.13,neg:.12,serif:.14,script:.15,yellow:.12}, gOutlineOn:false, gShadowOn:false, start:0}}` (if `_agent/caption-presets.json` has a "glorious" entry, use those caption values instead — her saved version has the shadow on). Leave `speed` out (1x) unless she asks.
8. **Zooms:** set `whipAt` to 3-5 big lines (~every 20-25s: "And here's the thing", the core reframe, the punchline), then run `python3 _agent/glorious.py autozoom <project>`. It plans the approved Captions-style zooms (see below).
9. **Render:** `G="$HOME/mnt/AI agent video edit/_agent/glorious.py"; python3 "$G" plan <project>` (prints the caption pages; read them like a viewer and fix keywords/words if a page reads badly), then `python3 "$G" chunk <project> <wd> N` for every chunk (2-3 per call; the first chunk also makes the hook cut-out), then `python3 "$G" assemble <project> <wd> <wd>/final.mp4` (frame count must match the "expected" it prints).
10. **Check:** frames from each B-roll slot, a few caption pages (no line wider than the frame, keyword lines look right, nothing over her face), a card, a whip, and the hook (word behind her head, readable, rising in). Optionally measure zooms by ORB-matching final vs clean frames (scale per frame) and make sure there are no single-frame jumps.
11. **Deliver** `Videos/<name>/<name>_glorious.mp4` (next _v2, _v3 if it exists), `<name>_glorious_list.txt` (hook, slots, keywords, zooms, cards, B-roll %, speed), update the project file (`status:"rendered"`, `rendered`), and add/replace the usage-log line in CATALOG.md (`- <date>, <video file stem> (<topic>, Glorious): clip, clip, ...`). Move superseded versions into `_agent/tmp/` to keep `edited/` tidy. Reply briefly with a what-she-says/what's-on-screen table and that she can tweak everything in `Editors/B-roll Editor.html`.

## Speed (added 2026-10-21)
`project.speed` (1 / 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3; missing = 1x) is set by her with the Speed menu next to the play button in the editor; the preview plays at that speed and shows the final length. The renderer keeps every timing (captions, zooms, B-roll, hook) in the clean video's timeline, speeds up each chunk at the very end (cumulative frame rounding keeps chunks in sync) and the audio with atempo (pitch stays natural). Default is 1x: never speed a video up on your own.

## The Glorious captions (renderer + editor preview use identical rules)
- Inter Tight Bold #FCFCFC, no outline/shadow/box by default, centered, tight tracking (-1.7%) and leading; each word slides in from the left and fades up as she says it; first word of each caption supersized.
- Pages: split at punctuation and pauses >0.5s; balanced chunks of at most 5 words; a keyword ends its page and sits alone on line 2; a keyword that opens a phrase stands alone; never a lone word left after a keyword; lines wrap at `gLine` (72% of width) and a page never exceeds 2 lines (it splits). Single long words shrink to fit inside `gPad`. When line 2 is small text the line gap is wider so it never touches the big first word.
- Editor options she uses: sizes per style, side padding, line width, colors (main, script, mustard), outline and shadow (not on negative words), height on face / on B-roll; presets (Bold Pop, Clean, Elegant, Boxed, Glorious) show their names and can be saved back with "Save changes to …" (stored in `_agent/caption-presets.json`).

## The zooms (approved 2026-10-21; modeled on the Captions video at 0:21-0:25)
- A new framing on almost every caption page while her face is on screen (~every 1.5s): wide 1.0 / medium 1.12 / tight 1.24, keyword pages tight, never the same twice in a row, at least 1.1s apart.
- Each change snaps in 0.22s, or instantly on a jump cut within 0.35s (hides the cut); then the framing creeps in slowly (+1.5%/s).
- Whips on big lines: one-frame-ish white flash (~55%, 2 frames) + 1.55x snapping back to medium over 0.8s with the measured curve [1, .951, .805, .369, .147, .065, .027, .005, 0]. Opening: soft 1.35x pull-back, no flash.
- No zoom changes under B-roll; after B-roll she comes back on a fresh wide/medium framing. Zoom is centered at (0.515, 0.5).
- Lessons: rare/slow zooms (every 8-10s) and subtle drifts were rejected — the fast per-phrase rhythm is the point. When studying a reference video built on an earlier edit of ours, compare against OUR clean video, not the intermediate (the intermediate already contained zooms and cancels them out).

## Rendering her edits from the editor
When she says "render my edits for <name>": read `Videos/<name>/editor files/<name>_broll_project.json` (status should be `edited`). First diff it against the last rendered project (caption settings, words/keywords, slots, zooms, cards, hook, speed) so you can tell her exactly what changed. Render it as-is with chunk/assemble (her edits are the plan; `chain_beats` keeps her zoom edits smooth; the hook cut-out is remade automatically if she changed the hook timing). Check newly chosen clips against the usage-log rule and mention a conflict rather than silently changing her pick. Deliver the next `_vN`, set status back to `rendered`, update the usage-log line, and reply with a short table of what changed.

## Don'ts
No captions/zooms/B-roll in step 1. Don't change the speed unless she set it in the editor or asked. Don't draw brand logos. Strip metadata (the renderer's assemble does).
